# Good LLM Ideas

Working narrative ideas selected or developed through LLM-assisted brainstorming. This is an incubation folder, not a declaration of game canon.

## Contents

- [original_notes.md](original_notes.md) - the complete former `docs/brainstorms/good_llm_ideas.md`, moved here unchanged. Its chronology, repetitions, disagreements, and superseded premises are intentionally preserved.
- [gates_morality_and_the_nazi_embassy.md](gates_morality_and_the_nazi_embassy.md) - standalone synthesis of moral consideration, the Golden Rule, the gate revolution, humanitarian diplomacy, and the Nazi embassy plot.

## Reading these drafts

The original notes are raw source material. The focused synthesis distinguishes the current direction from earlier candidates, especially the newer decision that AI is established infrastructure and gates are the present disruption. A proposal in either document is not automatically an approved implementation or final plot.

For dialogue, follow the [anti-LLMism style guide](../../concepts/anti-llmism-style-guide.md). See the broader [brainstorms index](../README.md).
