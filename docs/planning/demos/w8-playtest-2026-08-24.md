# W8 playtest — Jon's findings, 2026-08-24

The Smash campaign's W8 was *"Jon plays it"*, and it has now happened. This file
is the RECORD of what he said, kept verbatim below, plus the routing. It is not a
campaign: four findings are actionable, everything else is explicitly deferred.

⛔⛔ **THE MOST IMPORTANT LINE IN IT IS THE ONE THAT SAYS *STOP POLISHING*.** The
temptation after a playtest is a broad presentation pass, and Jon ruled that out
by name: VFX refinement, HUD animation tuning, animation cleanup across the cast,
timing/juice adjustments, another presentation audit. The bar is *"obvious
gameplay/readability defect → fix now; missing major genre/system capability →
implement now; merely could look nicer → defer."*

## The four actionable findings

```text
Forward Smash pre-movement        → FIX     (D204)
Robot v3 Pogo must stay in Smash  → KEEP    (D205)
Pointed Up-B concept/read         → REWORK  (D206)
active-match Exit / No Contest    → ADD     (D207)
```

## Two decisions closed by this message

- **stature** — there is NO standard adult height, and `ADULT_HEIGHT` must not
  exist. Robot v3 stays ~48 and reads intentionally SHORTER than most. The
  earlier normalization pass pulled too many characters toward his size, making
  the cast generally too small. ⛔ do not mechanically scale every 48 character
  up by one ratio; give characters intentional relative stature and leave the
  ambiguous ones alone until visually reviewed. (Decision 32.)
- **Kernel Guide** gets its own `CharacterDefinition`, because character identity
  is not sprite identity. It may borrow another presentation through the normal
  presentation mechanism. ⛔ do not invent a combat kit to fill the definition.

## Jon's message, verbatim

> Current maintainer/playtest feedback. Treat this as the authoritative direction
> for the next slice.
>
> ## 1. Forward Smash currently moves the fighter too much before the Smash starts
>
> This is a real gameplay defect.
>
> When I quickly perform a Forward Smash, the fighter currently travels
> noticeably before the Forward Smash takes over. For a quick Smash input, there
> should be little to no locomotion before startup. I should not effectively dash
> first and then Smash.
>
> Desired feel:
>
> ```text
> quick forward + strong attack gesture
>         ↓
> Forward Smash wins immediately
>         ↓
> little/no traversal displacement
>         ↓
> authored Smash startup
> ```
>
> A deliberately delayed input may legitimately move first:
>
> ```text
> hold/move forward
>         ↓
> later press attack
>         ↓
> movement already happened
> ```
>
> Do not fix this by cosmetically teleporting the fighter back or by adding an
> arbitrary giant root window after the move begins. Find the semantic ordering
> issue: Smash recognition should beat ordinary traversal/dash consumption early
> enough that the same input does not first become movement and then become a
> Smash.
>
> This sounds related to the previous discovery that shield+direction could still
> fall through to the dash one layer below the obvious resolver. Inspect the
> actual production chain from raw/canonical input through gesture resolution and
> movement intent.
>
> Add a direct regression using the real input path:
>
> ```text
> neutral fighter
> → quick forward-Smash gesture
> → advance through move recognition
> → assert Forward Smash starts
> → assert displacement before startup is negligible
> ```
>
> Also retain a control case where ordinary forward movement without the Smash
> gesture still moves normally.
>
> Do not tune this from CPU-vs-CPU emergent matches.
>
> ## 2. Robot v3 should keep Pogo in Smash
>
> This is intentional character identity.
>
> `robot_v3` should have Pogo available in Smash.
>
> **Do not make Pogo a universal Smash action.**
>
> The desired model remains:
>
> ```text
> character capabilities / prepared fighter kit
>              ↓
> Smash adaptation
>              ↓
> effective MovesetContract
> ```
>
> Robot v3 has Pogo because Robot v3 owns that capability. Another fighter
> without Pogo should not acquire one merely by entering Smash.
>
> This is also a useful test of the recently cleaned-up fighter-kit architecture:
> character-specific vocabulary should survive match preparation without
> requiring a stage-global fallback.
>
> Add/keep a test proving:
>
> ```text
> Robot v3 Smash fighter
> → Pogo available
>
> unrelated fighter without Pogo
> → Pogo absent
> ```
>
> Prefer preserving the existing Robot v3 Pogo semantics rather than inventing a
> Smash-specific duplicate implementation.
>
> ## 3. Pointed Polygon Up-B needs a different coarse shape
>
> The current Up-B does a good job of **bringing the victim with Pointed**, so
> preserve the useful autolink/carry behavior.
>
> Visually and geometrically, however, it does not currently read as an Up-B.
>
> Desired rough prototype:
>
> ```text
>          sword ─────►
>              \  |  /
>                Pointed
>              /  |  \
> >         ◄───── sword
>
>        broad spinning disk
>        around the fighter
>              ↑
>         upward recovery
> ```
>
> More concretely:
>
> * Pointed extends her swords approximately horizontally.
> * The attack volume should form a broad disk / horizontal spinning envelope
>   around her, rather than reading like a narrow ordinary strike.
> * She rises during it as the recovery.
> * Victims caught reasonably near the body should be carried with her through
>   the multihits.
> * The final hit can launch.
>
> Animation quality is **not** the priority yet.
>
> For the prototype, it is acceptable to fake the spin by repeatedly flipping the
> sprite horizontally:
>
> ```text
> normal
> flipped
> normal
> flipped
> ...
> ```
>
> if that gives the basic rotational read.
>
> Prioritize:
>
> ```text
> 1. hitbox geometry
> 2. victim carry/autolink
> 3. upward recovery motion
> 4. rough sword-horizontal visual pose
> 5. crude sprite flip animation
> 6. polished animation/VFX later
> ```
>
> Do not spend a lot of time producing beautiful spin animation yet.
>
> Also preserve the structural autolink correction from review: an authored
> Pointed-local anchor must be resolved using **Pointed's frame/facing at the hit
> producer**, not reconstructed later from the victim's gravity or knockback-away
> direction.
>
> Useful direct tests:
>
> ```text
> victim near Pointed's left side
> → multihit catches/carries
>
> victim near Pointed's right side
> → multihit catches/carries
>
> victim somewhat above/below center
> → broad disk still reads sensibly
>
> Pointed faces either direction
> → geometry mirrors from Pointed's facing
>
> different attacker/victim gravity frames
> → autolink anchor follows Pointed's frame
> ```
>
> The rough gameplay feel is more important than exact final hitbox dimensions
> right now.
>
> ## 4. Match system menu needs `Exit Match` / `No Contest`
>
> During an active Smash match, the system/pause menu needs an explicit:
>
> ```text
> Exit Match
> ```
>
> which ends the match as:
>
> ```text
> No Contest
> ```
>
> It should not award an ordinary winner/loser result.
>
> Desired flow:
>
> ```text
> active match
> → pause/system menu
> → Exit Match
> → confirmation if the existing menu conventions call for one
> → terminate match
> → result = No Contest
> → return to the appropriate Smash frontend/select flow
> ```
>
> Reuse the existing match outcome / route transition machinery. Do not introduce
> a one-off scene teardown path.
>
> If `No Contest` is not currently an explicit outcome, add it at the semantic
> match-outcome layer rather than encoding it as some special winner value.
>
> This should also work in CPU-vs-CPU and other roster configurations; it is a
> match-level command, not a player-body action.
>

> ## 8. Defense presentation direction
>

>
> ```text

>             ↓
> route/game presentation policy
>             ↓
> opt-in shared cues
> ```

>
> ```text

> ## 9. Preserve the recent architecture/correctness wins
>
> Do not reopen without a targeted regression:
>
> * D202's single post-publication control restriction phase.
> * Damage-only hits preserving existing velocity.
> * Actor/player shared accepted-hit reaction.
> * Ordinary hits not restoring spent double jumps.
> * Cause-specific air-resource refresh.
> * Capture and ledge catch restoring the appropriate resources.
> * Ground-only shield not remaining active after entering air.
> * Deterministic same-tick capture matching.
> * Strike-pulse one-hit-per-victim accounting.
> * Buffered Special semantic intent.
> * Explicit Smash charge hold points.
> * `MoveCharge` as charge-payoff authority.
> * Match-level rollback-safe impact hitstop.
> * Pointed's canonical authored-facing transform.
> * Prepared fighter-kit authority.
> * Shared move-authoring primitives.
>
> ## 10. Outstanding correctness findings from the latest review
>
> Before adding much more parity, ensure these are actually closed in current
> HEAD:
>
> ### Fresh/reset recovery budget
>
> A fresh or stock-respawned airborne body must start with its intended recovery
> charge. Do not rely on a later landing event to initialize it.
>
> Test the real stock-respawn road:
>
> ```text
> lose stock
> → respawn airborne
> → before landing
> → recovery Up-B is available once
> ```
>
> ### Respawn-grace ownership
>
> Respawn protection must not borrow/overwrite the entire generic `Empowered`
> component.
>
> The ownership test must use **the same body**:
>
> ```text
> existing empowerment
> +
> respawn grace
> → attack
> → respawn grace removed
> → unrelated empowerment survives
> ```
>
> Prefer a dedicated respawn grant/reason rather than pretending `RespawnGrace`
> owns another system's whole component.
>
> ### Autolink source frame
>
> Pointed-local/autolink anchors must be transformed from the **attacker's
> frame/facing** at the producer. Do not use victim gravity or `HitKnockback.dir`
> as a substitute for attacker facing.
>
> This is particularly relevant while reworking Pointed's Up-B.
