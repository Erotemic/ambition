> **RE-VERIFIED 2026-09-03. ⭐ ALL FOUR CLOSURES HAVE SOMETHING HOLDING THEM
> SHUT, which is the question a closed receipt should answer and this one did
> not.** "Fixed" and "cannot come back" are different claims, and a playtest
> receipt is exactly where the difference goes unnoticed.
>
> | finding | what stops it returning |
> |---|---|
> | startup movement | `MoveSpec::rooted_by_charge` (`combat/src/moveset/mod.rs:806`), guarded both ways — `a_charge_whose_policy_does_not_root_leaves_the_body_its_steering` and `a_rooted_move_latches_the_stick_the_player_is_holding_not_the_one_it_may_move_by` |
> | Pogo as repertoire | character-specific authoring, live in the actor content |
> | Up-B geometry | `character_sprites/src/attack_hitbox/tests.rs`, plus the strike geometry in `combat/src/strike.rs` |
> | Exit Match / no-contest | `features/stocks_match.rs` and `combat/src/stocks.rs`, and it is snapshot state (`snapshot_impls.rs`), so a schema change cannot drop it silently |
>
> ⚠ The first row is the one worth knowing about, because it is the finding the
> receipt itself calls the most reusable lesson: the rooting policy is guarded in
> BOTH directions — a move that roots and a move that deliberately does not. A
> guard that only pins the fixed case would have let the opposite regression
> through.

A fifth identity clarification from the same review established that the Kernel
Guide is a character identity, not a special body-kit archetype.

The most reusable architectural lesson is the first one: apparent input-ordering
symptoms can be caused later by one control path failing to receive the same move
motion/rooting authority as another. Reproduce through the real input/control
road before changing gesture semantics.

The playtest also explicitly deferred broad polish that merely "could look
nicer"—cast-wide animation cleanup, VFX/HUD juice and presentation tuning without
a concrete wrong behavior. That remains a useful proof-of-concept prioritization
rule, not an open W8 task.

The original verbatim report and implementation chronology remain in git history.
