# Persistent actors and population

**State:** MERGED into the open-world/reconstitution programs.

Persistent actors do not need a separate actor ontology. They need the same
explicit distinctions as other authoritative occurrences:

- semantic identity/provenance;
- current residency/location;
- durable disposition when product policy requires it;
- reconstruction from authored content plus durable facts;
- optional background simulation policy independent of mere existence.

Current design questions are owned by
[`open-world-runtime-and-residency.md`](open-world-runtime-and-residency.md) and
[`construction-and-reconstitution.md`](construction-and-reconstitution.md).
Navigation/agent policy remains in
[`platformer-navigation-and-reachability.md`](platformer-navigation-and-reachability.md).

Do not create a universal persistent-NPC manager merely to replace the former
planning file.
