## Lane D — Jon's observations

The source is
[`JONS_OBSERVATIONS_BUGS_AND_ISSUES.md`](JONS_OBSERVATIONS_BUGS_AND_ISSUES.md)
and **that file's own rule governs it**: an agent edits it only to mark something
as *potentially* done; Jon removes the line or says it is not actually solved.
Rows here are the architecture reads, not a second copy of the list.

**Three of them are architecture wearing gameplay clothes**, and they compete
with Lane C on merit:

- ▢ **D1. Dormant actors — an engine concept, and Jon says so.** *"We probably
  need an engine concept that allows actors to be dormant… this sort of
  optimization will likely be generally important for any game using the engine,
  although it's not something that should be inherent."* AI slop walks off the
  level before Mary-O arrives. ⚠ **the non-inherent half is the design**: split
  screen and network multiplayer break a camera-proximity rule, and some
  characters never sleep. Dormancy is a POLICY an actor declares, not a distance
  the engine assumes — and it is rollback state the moment it gates a brain.
- ▢ **D2. One reset, at one expressible time.** *"When maryo dies the enemies
  seem to reset before the death animation is finished. The level reset needs to
  happen all at once at a time that is easy to express in the game code."* Jon
  flags it may be structural and warns against a monolith. This is the
  resolve/validate/commit shape the match-activation transaction already took —
  a reset is a transaction with an authored trigger point, not a scatter of
  systems each choosing its own moment.
- ▢ **D3. A reset that leaves the previous run's drops standing.** *"When I reset
  the level old drops from enemies still seem to be there."* Same authority as D2
  and probably the same fix: an entity spawned by an event outlives the lifecycle
  that owned its spawner. ⚠ related to B3 — a spawned item nothing owns is the
  same shape as a spawned item nothing snapshots.
- ▢ **D4. Hall of characters: humanoids are dramatically out of scale.** Jon asks
  for it **at the generator level, not a post-hoc fix**, and notes player robot v3
  is chibi ON PURPOSE. ⚠ the Hall is GENERATED and is a stress test — the standing
  rule is *when it is wrong, fix the engine or the generator, never the level*.
- ▢ **D5. Mary-O's gameplay box is her raw alpha silhouette.** Her generator
  authors no `body_inset`, so hat and arms are in the collision box and her tall
  form is ~36px against a 32px tile. Seven other characters override it; the seam
  is fractional so it survives art changes. ⛔ **not a second box authority in the
  game.** Residue: pixel→world scale has no representation in the builder at all.
- ▢ **D6. The combat feel cluster** — Sanic gets no i-frames, hitstun or knockback
  on hit and his rings barely splash; super-Sanic is not invincible to spikes;
  Mary-O is hit during her death animation; her downgrade is instant where it
  should be a beat. These are one seam (hit response → stun/i-frames/knockback,
  already the authoritative kernel `ambition_platformer2d_core::hit_response`),
  not four bugs. Ring recollection after hitstun is a real design constraint —
  they must outlive the stun.
- ▢ **D7. Pickups are magnetic and should not be.** *"Maryo coins and sanic rings
  should not be magnetic to the player."* One attraction rule, two games.
- ▢ **D8. Mary-O's fireball is tiny and only fires right.** Size, and it must
  follow her facing — a fixed +x is a body-local vector treated as world.
- ▢ **D9. Mary-O flashes when her fireball hits an enemy.** The striker is
  reading the victim's feedback; the same class as the shared-reason invulnerability
  work.
- ▢ **D10. She can stand on a broken brick.** The collider outlives the break.
- ▢ **D11. Used blocks need their own texture and a nudge animation** — an
  in-code position nudge up and back, so a spent block reads as spent.
- ▢ **D12. World 1-2 is a LEVEL, not a detour.** It follows a 1-1 win, cycles back
  to 1-1 when beaten, needs vertical elevator platforms that wrap when they leave
  the view, and needs enemies — Jon asks for the flying snakes on a plane. ⚠ the
  wrap is an engine-shaped question (a platform that teleports is a transit, and
  transits have an arbiter).
- ▢ **D13. The web build cannot use kaleidoscope** — lunex has no wasm support.
  Degrade the effect on wasm rather than dropping the build.
