So here's a fun fact about Ocarina of Time. Koji Kondo was worried that people would get sick of the overworld music, if every time they left an area and returned to Hyrule Field the same music was playing. So what he did was create a whole bunch of eight-measure components (around 20 or so), with the same chord progression, set up to play randomly one after the other, with each component ending in a way that would lead nicely into any random segment that happened to follow it. He divided these into three groups, for regular moving around, battling, and standing still, so that the music that played would match the actions Link was taking on screen. This means every time you travel through Hyrule Field a unique composition will end up playing, informed by the way you play the game (a lot of standing still will result in more of the standing still segments playing, and so on). In a 2011 Iwata asks interview, Kondo stated "Not many people noticed."
